          Requirements

          Each requirement is live or archived by one fixed rule, and says why. Nothing is deleted.

          How the archive rule works
          1. M archived from the dashboard: someone archived it with the button on the row, with a reason. The page can only say which dashboard session clicked, not who. A restore keeps it live whatever the rules say. Both are recorded (session, when, why) and neither deletes anything. Your own requirements archived this way are counted on the Overview until you review them.
          2. R1 terminal state: the record says resolved, closed, done, superseded, expired, declined or withdrawn.
          3. R2 done live: a deploy and a live test for the same ticket or commit, the newest of each by time, the test passing and not older than the deploy; or its ticket is closed. A local test pass, a lone deploy or a failing test never archives.
          4. R3 superseded: a newer record in the same session has exactly the same words and is itself live. Near-duplicates stay live.
          5. R4 expired: a requirement over 30 days old with no owner, ticket or evidence that is not sourced to you (the 30 days is an assumption). Anything you said yourself is never archived by age. An ask is never archived by age or deadline: an ask past its deadline stays in Needs you.

          Anything else is live. The first matching rule wins, and a reopened ticket or newer failing evidence brings the item back on the next read.

          Archive

          Asks and requirements that are resolved, closed, superseded or expired, with their provenance and resolution. Viewing only: no record is deleted.

          Chat reaches your agents labelled as dashboard text. It can start or steer ordinary work. It never counts as an approval.
