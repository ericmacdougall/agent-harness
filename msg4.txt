fix(videos): red-team fixes for Prev/Next, short windows, focus

Why: Red-team review of PR #7 found that tapping Next after the current lesson was hidden by a progress filter (for example just marked watched under Unwatched) jumped back to the first visible lesson; a short desktop window also got the phone layout; and resizing to desktop with the sheet open dropped focus to the body.
What: Previous and Next now go to the nearest visible lesson after or before the current one in catalog order when the current lesson is filtered out. The short-landscape clause of the phone query now requires a coarse pointer (CSS and MOBILE_QUERY). Leaving the phone layout with the sheet open focuses the search box. Add checks for all three.
